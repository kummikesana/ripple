Privacy

Plain language, no legal maze. Here's exactly what Ripple does with your information.

If you're just browsing

Nothing leaves your device. Your garden, your tries, everything, lives only in your browser's local storage. We never see it, and it's gone if you clear your browser data.

Asking the crew something new

Most questions on the Ask page are answered instantly from a list we wrote ourselves, nothing leaves your device for those. If your question doesn't match one of those and the crew "thinks it through live," the text you typed is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate an answer. We don't attach your name, email or account to that request, and we don't keep a copy of it ourselves. We don't control what Google does with it on their end, the same as any app that calls a third-party AI service.

What we store if you sign in

Just two things: your email address, and your Ripple garden data (your progress, switches you're trying, facts you've played, and similar). That's the whole list.

Why we store it

So your garden can follow you to any device you sign into, instead of staying stuck in one browser. Signing in is entirely optional and never required to use Ripple.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase, in an EU region (Paris where available). Access is locked down so only you can read or change your own data.

No ads, no selling

We don't run ads, and we don't sell or rent your data to anyone. The only time anything you type leaves Ripple to a third party is the live-answer feature described above, and that's to generate an answer for you, not to profile or track you. Ripple makes no money from your information.

Deleting your account

Go to My garden → Account → Delete my account any time. This permanently removes your email and garden data from our servers, right away, and can't be undone.
